Buying tips

  • Pick a probe bandwidth at least 3–5× the fastest edge you need to see.
  • Check the voltage rating against your peak voltage, including spikes.
  • For current, check both the continuous (rms) and peak rating.
  • Buy from an Indian supplier for a GST invoice and warranty support.
