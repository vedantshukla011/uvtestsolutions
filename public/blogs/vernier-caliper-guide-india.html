What is a vernier caliper?

A vernier caliper measures outside dimensions, inside dimensions, depth and steps using a main scale and a sliding vernier scale. Typical ranges are 0–150 mm, 0–200 mm and 0–300 mm, with larger sizes for heavy engineering.

What is the least count of a vernier caliper?

Least count = value of one main-scale division ÷ number of vernier divisions. For a common metric caliper with 1 mm main-scale divisions and 50 vernier divisions, the least count is 1 ÷ 50 = 0.02 mm.

How to read a vernier caliper

  1. Close the jaws and confirm the zero lines align (check zero error).
  2. Read the main scale just left of the vernier zero.
  3. Find the vernier line that aligns exactly with a main-scale line.
  4. Reading = main-scale reading + (aligned vernier line × least count).

Buying checklist

  • Range and resolution that match your parts and tolerances.
  • Genuine brand — counterfeit calipers are common in online marketplaces.
  • Calibration certificate for ISO / IATF audits.
  • IP rating (e.g. coolant-proof digital models) for machining areas.
  • GST invoice and warranty from an Indian supplier.
