What is a bidirectional DC power supply?

A bidirectional (regenerative) DC power supply can both source power to a device and sink power from it. When sinking, Wocen units feed the energy back to the grid instead of burning it as heat. This saves electricity, cuts cooling needs, and lets one instrument replace a separate power supply and electronic load.

Buying checklist

  • Maximum voltage, current and power you need — including future headroom.
  • Whether you need sink (load) mode and energy recovery.
  • Response time for dynamic or battery-simulation tests.
  • Parallel expansion if your power needs will grow.
  • Indian supplier with GST invoice, installation and application support.
